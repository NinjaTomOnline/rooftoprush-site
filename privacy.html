NinjaTom: Rooftop Rush privacy

Current launch build position: no account requirement, no third-party analytics, no ad tracking, and no access to location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

Last updated April 30, 2026 No Account Required No Ad Tracking

Data Collection

The current app build does not require an account and does not include third-party analytics, advertising tracking, location access, camera access, microphone access, or contact access.

On-Device Data

Gameplay settings and progress such as best distance, score, accessibility preferences, and tutorial state are stored locally on the device using iOS app storage.

Support Requests

If you contact support, the information you choose to send, such as your email address, device model, screenshots, or issue description, is used only to respond to that support request.

Future Changes

If future versions add online services, cloud saves, analytics, or purchases, this policy and the App Store privacy answers should be updated before that release ships.
