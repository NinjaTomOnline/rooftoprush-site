The campaign idea

Style is not cosmetic. Style is the weapon.

Every charge spent should feel like NinjaTom sacrificed a piece of the look to stop worse fashion from spreading. Every shopping bag should feel like the fit snapping back into place.

01

Throw the fit

Spend Style Charges by hurling the fedora, shades, tie, coat, and other pieces of NinjaTom's look at bad-fashion threats.

02

Restock the swagger

Shopping bags reload the wardrobe and keep the character silhouette readable while the run keeps moving.

03

Expose the empire

Evidence pickups push chapters forward through counterfeit warehouses, elite henchmen, and the rescue-Memphis chase.
