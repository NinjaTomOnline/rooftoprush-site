A FEW PERFECTLY REASONABLE QUESTIONS

Before you throw your hat.

Are these games the mobile game?

No. Fedora Flick and Memphis Sniff Squad are free browser side games set in the same world. Rooftop Rush is the larger iPhone and iPad runner.

Can I join TestFlight?

The current beta is private and available to the existing internal team. There is no public invitation link yet. Release news will appear here.

Do I need an account?

No. The arcade saves best scores in this browser only. You can clear them from either game. It has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Does it work on my phone?

The browser games support touch and keyboard controls. Fedora Flick includes adjustable aim, pause and reduced-motion support. Sniff Squad has no timer.
